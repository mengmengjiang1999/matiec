## Why

MATIEC has no namespace AST or resolution model, while public multi-vendor material
shows a modern language family that needs evidence before syntax is frozen.

## What Changes

- Establish an evidence-backed namespace and qualified-name model.
- Add profile-gated parsing and deterministic resolution diagnostics.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `namespace-resolution`: Namespace declarations, qualified names, and lookup.

## Impact

Lexer, grammar, AST, symbol tables, semantic resolution, generators, tests, and docs.
